About LPC Repairs

Your trusted local specialists for consumer electronics maintenance.

Our Story

Founded with a commitment to high-quality technical craftsmanship, LPC Repairs specializes in fixing three primary device pillars: Laptops, Phones, and Consoles. Whether it's a student's broken laptop screen before exams, a smartphone with a damaged screen, or a gaming console suffering from HDMI faults, our workshop is equipped to handle complex component repairs.

Our Mission

To provide fast, reliable, and affordable repair solutions while reducing electronic waste through sustainable device maintenance and transparent customer service.

Quality Standards

  • Certified technicians using premium Grade-A replacement parts.
  • Strict data protection and privacy policies while servicing devices.
  • Clear and honest diagnostic communication before work begins.
